Stays on device

Data stored on your device

Every puzzle you play, every result, streak, and stat is written to your device's local storage (AsyncStorage) and stays there.

It is never uploaded to us, and no one else can read it. Uninstall Crunch and all of it is deleted with the app.

Leaves anonymously

Analytics

Crunch uses PostHog for anonymous product analytics. Events such as completing a puzzle or tapping share are recorded, so we can see which parts of the game work and which need fixing.

No personally identifiable information is captured, and IP addresses are not stored. Nothing in an event traces back to you. To stop sending events entirely, uninstall the app.

Leaves anonymously

Third party services

Each day's puzzle is served from Supabase, which hosts the puzzle data. The app asks for a puzzle and gets one back.

Only puzzle data is fetched. No game history, no stats, and nothing about you is sent to Supabase.

Never collected

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, or any other personally identifiable information.

There is no account system, so there is nothing linking a game to a person — and no profile of you to share, sell, or lose.

Everything else

Children

Crunch is suitable for all ages — it is a target, six numbers, and a timer. We do not knowingly collect data from children. As we collect no identifying data from anyone, we hold none about a child either.
